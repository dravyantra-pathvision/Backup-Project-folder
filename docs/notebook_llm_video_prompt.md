# Notebook LLM Prompt: Gitam Interns Showcase Video

**Instructions for Use:**
Copy and paste the prompt below into Notebook LLM to generate the script and scene suggestions for your 1-minute closing ceremony video.

---

**Prompt:**

Act as an expert video producer and scriptwriter. Please generate a highly engaging, fast-paced 1-minute video script for a closing ceremony event. The video's purpose is to celebrate and showcase the incredible work done by our team of 5 talented interns from GITAM University who worked on the "DravYantra" (Fleet Management & IoT) project. 

The video must highlight their specific contributions dynamically:
1. **The Backend Team (2 Interns):** Highlight how they architected and built the entire robust backend for the Fleet Owner App from scratch, enabling real-time data flow and scalable architecture.
2. **The Hardware Research Team (2 Interns):** Showcase their hands-on research and development of the IoT hardware, sensors, and telemetry integration that power our vehicle tracking.
3. **The Data Analyst (1 Intern):** Feature their work on building the comprehensive analytics dashboard, transforming raw data into actionable insights for fleet owners.

**Requirements for the output:**
- Total runtime should be strictly around 60 seconds (approx. 130-150 words of voiceover).
- Format the output with two columns (or clear sections) for **Visual/On-Screen Action** and **Audio/Voiceover**.
- Tone: Inspiring, high-energy, proud, and tech-forward. 
- Include a strong opening hook and a celebratory closing thanking the GITAM interns for their dedication and impact on the DravYantra project.
- Suggest background music style (e.g., upbeat electronic, cinematic tech).

Please provide the complete script and visual directions.
