SECTION 03

3. Hardware Telematics & IoT Sensor Compliance

DravYantra PathVision interfaces seamlessly with a broad array of automotive telematics hardware and onboard sensors. Hardware deployment is subject to strict engineering operational standards:

Certified Telematics Device Specifications:

• AIS-140 Certified GPS Trackers: Dual-SIM cellular 4G/LTE telematics units compliant with Indian Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) standards, featuring internal SOS emergency buttons and 10-second packet interval polling.
• OBD-II Diagnostic Adapters: Plug-and-play vehicle bus telemetry modules extracting real-time vehicle speed (km/h), engine RPM, coolant temperature, ignition voltage, and diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs).
• Digital Fuel Level Sensors: RS485 ultrasonic tank probes, capacitive fuel rods, and float-arm voltage transducers installed inside primary and secondary fuel tanks.
• Engine Relay Actuators: Remote immobilizer relays wired to vehicle starter/ignition circuits for automated or admin-triggered engine cutoff during critical security events.

3.1 Installation Liability & Certification

All physical hardware installations, vehicle wiring splices, and sensor calibrations must be conducted by certified automotive electrical engineers. DravYantra accepts zero financial or legal liability for vehicle electrical short circuits, battery drain, ECU failures, or vehicle OEM warranty voids caused by improper third-party wiring or unauthorized hardware tampering.

Warning on Fuel Tank Calibration:

Fuel theft detection sensitivity relies on initial tank calibration curves (Voltage/RS485 raw value vs. Fuel Liters). Uncalibrated sensors or irregular tank shapes may yield percentage variances. DravYantra does not guarantee 100% loss prevention against physical fuel siphoning under uncalibrated states.

SECTION 04

4. Software Infrastructure, Cloud Architecture & SLA

The DravYantra platform operates on an enterprise-grade cloud architecture designed for continuous telemetry processing:

  • Cloud Compute & Database: High-concurrency Node.js REST API servers deployed on Amazon Web Services (AWS EC2) with PostgreSQL relational database clusters and indexed spatial queries.
  • Encrypted Document Repository: Amazon Web Services S3 cloud bucket infrastructure (dravyantra-uploads) enforcing server-side AES-256 encryption for vehicle RCs, driver licenses, and fleet compliance artifacts.
  • GIS Location Rendering: High-definition CARTO Voyager basemap layers providing international English (Latin) location labels across all tracking views.

4.1 Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Cellular Connectivity

DravYantra targets a 99.9% operational uptime SLA for core API gateways and live map tracking web sockets. However, telematics data transmission requires active cellular GSM coverage (4G/LTE/5G) and GNSS satellite visibility (GPS/GLONASS/NavIC). DravYantra shall not be held liable for telemetry packet delays or temporary signal dropouts occurring in underground parking structures, mountainous tunnels, cellular dead zones, or during severe solar/atmospheric interference.
